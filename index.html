working systems.

A mix of production engineering, computer vision, and applied research.

I'm an ML and Computer Vision Engineer with 1+ year of industry experience, building end-to-end machine learning and computer vision solutions.

Currently, I work at Sports for Life (SFL), where I build and improve end-to-end sports analytics pipelines for football, covering video processing, player and ball tracking, jersey number recognition, event analysis, player statistics, and automated match highlights generation.

Previously, I worked as a Computer Vision Engineer at Mowito Automation, where I developed vision-based solutions for industrial robotic applications, including defect detection, anomaly detection, and OCR-based inspection.

I completed my M.Tech in Robotics and Mobility Systems from IIT Jodhpur, where my research focused on video understanding and multimodal learning. I also co-authored a research paper on underwater pipeline detection using classical computer vision techniques.
